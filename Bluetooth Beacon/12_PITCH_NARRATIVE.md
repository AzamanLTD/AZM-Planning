# 12 — Partner and investor narrative

## One-line description

**Azaman Presence connects physical merchant locations to approved digital storefronts through a privacy-conscious BLE proximity layer in the Azaman mobile app.**

## The problem

Customers often search manually to find a participating business or its menu, catalogue, guest service or marketplace entry point. Merchants may have a physical presence but no simple connection between that moment and their Azaman storefront.

## Proposition

A small BLE beacon broadcasts a registered location identifier. An opted-in Azaman customer's phone detects it and resolves the branch through Azaman backend. App offers the merchant's approved experience.

Merchant does not need Wi-Fi, Ethernet, gateway or SIM for base discovery. Customer phone uses own network connection.

## Why it matters

Azaman could connect physical branches, verified device assignments, approved storefronts, customer preferences/contextual discovery, existing orders/bookings/tickets/payments and merchant operations/consent-based engagement.

Differentiation is the merchant network and service/payment integrations—not BLE hardware alone.

## Five verticals

- Retail: catalogue and offers.
- Restaurants: menus and ordering.
- Hotels: reservations and guest services.
- Fuel: station discovery, explicit pump choice and authorised payment workflow.
- Transit: operator, stop and route context, then ticketing integration.

## Rollout

Prove detection and storefront resolution in retail/restaurants. Extend to hotels/transit when integrations are ready. Develop fuel with OMC partner under separate safety/payment gates.

## What can be said today

BLE is a short-range broadcast technology. Proposed architecture can link observations to existing merchant branches. Candidate suppliers publish standalone BLE products. Concept can be evaluated with a limited sample and controlled tests.

## What must not be claimed yet

Universal background detection; exact distance/table/floor/pump from RSSI; beacon authentication of customer/location; continuous health without telemetry; fuel phone-use/device safety; provider supports POS/dispense integration; proven footfall/conversion; approved bulk order/live fuel payment.

## Partner ask

Retail/restaurant pilot: few branches, permission to install/test approved model, merchant/staff participation, opt-in session measurement, false-detection feedback, support/replacement/removal agreement.

Fuel ask is separate: OMC sponsorship, site-safety review, device/location approval, POS/forecourt integration and payment/reconciliation collaboration.
